# 05. Continue a roadmap starts every ready stage

## What to build

*Continue a roadmap* starts **every** ready stage of the roadmap, up to the same
limit, the press standing in for whatever would otherwise have started them.

**The card names them all.** The roadmap rows under *Other actions*, the adopting
Conversation's pane and the compose page all named *the Stage that would be
started*; they name every stage the press would start, so what the human is
offered is what pressing does.

**The press starts them all.** The first becomes the Conversation the human
composed, exactly as it does today; the rest are started as Conversations of
their own, given the same Pairings, the same companions and the same base commit
the human settled on the composer — the press is the one act that settles all of
that, and a stage started beside the first has no draft moment of its own either.

**And the guard refusing a roadmap with a stage in flight goes.** A roadmap with
01 under way and 02 ready is continued for 02. What that guard protected
against — a second Conversation on a stage already under way — is still refused,
by the record saying the stage is in flight and by its branch being taken.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The roadmap card and the adoption pane name both stages the press would
      start, rather than the lower of them.
- [ ] The press starts both: one as this Conversation, the other as a
      Conversation of its own with the same Pairings, companions and base.
- [ ] A roadmap with 01 in flight and 02 ready is offered, and the press
      continues it for 02 alone.
