# 04. One stopped presentation

## What to build

One stopped shape in the workbench. A Conversation stopped because an account
ran out of window draws exactly what one stopped by a press draws: the same
Notice card saying what stopped and why, the same *blocked on you* badge, and
the same Resume. The reset time is drawn as text beside that button — words the
human reads, not a countdown — and it is the only thing that tells the two
apart.

So the Pause card goes, along with its wire type, the record of how a wait
ended and the styling underneath it. There is no *go on without waiting* and no
way to resume a Pause by its Event: there is one Resume, and it clears whatever
stopped the run.

The badge reads one thing now. With the Hold retired in task 01 and the two
stops merged in task 02, what a Conversation is blocked on is its stop.

**Pause Events already on Timelines still read.** They are the record of what
happened and nothing rewrites them, so a stored one draws as a dated Event
carrying the line the session printed, with nothing to press.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Conversation stopped by an exhausted window and one stopped by Stop
      draw the same card, the same badge and the same button, differing only
      in the reset words beside Resume.
- [ ] Nothing in the viewer resumes a Pause by its Event, and no card offers
      to go on without waiting.
- [ ] A Timeline holding a Pause Event written before this stage still reads,
      showing what the session printed.
