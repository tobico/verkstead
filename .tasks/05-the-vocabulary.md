# 05. The vocabulary this stage retires

## What to build

`CONTEXT.md` follows the code. **Halt** and **Pause** fold into one entry for
the one stop, named **Stopped** — the state, told apart in its own words from
**Stop**, which is the press. **Hold** goes, with nothing in its place.

Then follow the cross-references out, because more entries name these than
define them:

- **Stalled** is defined against both of the first two — a driven Conversation
  with nothing driving it, no Halt written down and no open Pause — and now
  has one thing to be defined against.
- **Notice** and **Resume** both name the Halt: what a stopped run waits on,
  and what answers it.
- **Timeline** and **Screen** carry the Hold's carve-outs — that watching
  leaves no Event, and that watching commits the human to nothing.
- **Manual Task** names the Halt it does not clear, and lists the hold among
  the words to avoid.

Say the parts of the one stop that the merged entry now has to carry on its
own: that it is deliberate or by circumstance and what a restart does with
each; that nothing advances past one; that nothing about it reverts, resets or
stashes anything; that an exhausted account is a wait rather than a reason to
spend a different Profile; and that no stop resumes itself, the reset time
being information rather than a timer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No entry describes a Hold, a self-resuming Pause, or two kinds of stop.
- [ ] No *Avoid* line sends a reader to a term this stage retired.
- [ ] The merged entry names the state, and reading it beside **Stop** leaves
      no doubt which is the state and which is the button.
