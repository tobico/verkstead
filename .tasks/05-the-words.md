# 05. The words

## What to build

What the project says about itself, brought up to a server that has places.

- **`CONTEXT.md`'s Stage** — *up to as many Stages of one roadmap at a time as
  Settings says* is one of two limits now. Every Stage whose dependencies have
  settled starts up to that limit **and up to four Conversations across the whole
  server**, whatever roadmap or Process those are. A Stage waiting for a place is
  a third way a Stage is neither started nor halted, beside waiting on a
  dependency and waiting to join.
- **A term for a place on the server**, in the glossary's own shape, saying what
  takes one — a Conversation with a session running or a driver registered, a
  grilling and a Review and a Tinker alike — and what does not: a Conversation
  that is Done, Draft or stopped, **the halted Stage included**, which keeps its
  place under its own roadmap's limit and holds none here. And that only what
  Verkstead starts by itself is ever held back: a press goes ahead over the limit
  and is counted from then on.
- **`CONTEXT.md`'s Adopt**, where the press is described as starting every ready
  Stage up to the limit — which limit, now that there are two.
- **`docs/design/verkstead.md`**, where stage 04 wrote *how many run together is
  a setting*. **Refined rather than rewritten**, in the document's own *refined
  <date>, building <stage>* form, the way every decision there is refined as the
  stage that changes it lands.

ADR-0021's *What starts, and how many* already says all of this and stays as it
is: the words here reference it rather than restating it.

**What is not written here** is how a Stage waiting for a place is drawn on the
stage card or the roadmap's details pane. That is stage 06's, which shows the
other two ways a Stage is not started; what this stage leaves it is the state
told apart and the Timeline saying so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The glossary's **Stage** and **Adopt** say there are two limits and which
      is which, in the glossary's own voice and with ADR-0021 referenced rather
      than restated.
- [ ] A place on the server has an entry of its own saying what takes one and
      what does not, the halted Stage's asymmetry between the two limits
      included.
- [ ] The design document is refined in its *refined <date>, building <stage>*
      form, with the superseded wording left standing in front of the refinement.
