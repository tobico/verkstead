# agy-harness

The Antigravity CLI — Google's `agy` — becomes the fifth harness a Profile can
name, at the parity ADR-0011 asks of every backend: launchable under a Profile
of its type, asking on the channel it can afford, judged idle off its own
screen, its Transcript followed and its conversation resumed across the
cluster, with a login Verkstead runs itself. The decisions are ADR-0011's
amendment of 2026-11-01 and ADR-0022's of the same date; the words are
CONTEXT.md's.

Two facts shape the order. Everything after the first task needs the human's
sign-in, so the login comes second; and agy keeps its token in a keyring that a
sandbox must be kept from reaching, which on a Mac and on Windows can only be
proved there.
