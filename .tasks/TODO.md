# Integrated direction

Fold the direction choice into the grilling's closing Set, and keep the
planning work in the grilling session. Today accepting a wrap-up proposal ends
the grilling session, the human walks back to the Timeline to press a chooser,
and a fresh agent rebuilds the plan from a handoff — losing the grilling
context that would have written it best. After this feature: the closing Set
carries the chooser, a pick returns to the still-living grilling session, the
session itself writes the backlog or roadmap (or the handoff, for inline),
and Verkstead moves on artifacts plus quiet rather than on the answer. The
Direction state leaves the ladder; the handoff is written after the choice
and only for inline.

Settled in the grilling session of 2026-08-23; the decision record is
docs/adr/0008-pick-informs-artifacts-move.md.
