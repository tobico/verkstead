# setup-wizard-rail

The setup wizard rebuilt to the approved *B · Rail* design: a rail of the
three steps beside the open step's paper from 60rem, folding into a header
with a Steps sheet on a phone; grouped rows and tiles on the first step, cards
on the second and third; a primary button that names what it does; and the
by-hand screen reachable without a run. The boards and the designer's notes are
under `docs/design/setup-wizard/`, and that README lists where the grilling
overrode the notes. Nothing about ADR-0016's rules changes except what the last
task writes down: the objective, the met-only reopening and the no-skip rule
all stand.

Grilled in Question Sets 1387 to 1391. Every decision those settled is in the
task that builds it, so a task needs nothing beyond this backlog, the design
folder, `CONTEXT.md`, `docs/copy.md` and the code.
