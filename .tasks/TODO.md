# A Gradle daemon of Verkstead's own

Either a Gradle build in a session runs through a daemon Verkstead started, in
a Sandbox of Verkstead's own, with the speed a daemon gives and no session's
build ever running inside another's Sandbox — or this stage ends with a written
finding that it cannot be done soundly, and the daemon stays off as stage 04
left it. Both are this stage finished.

Nothing here is designed yet. The stage opens with a spike that measures the
daemon of Verkstead's own and the per-session registry side by side, puts what
it found to the human, and only then writes whatever building the decision
calls for into this backlog, ahead of the docs.
