# The server-wide limit

Verkstead starts nothing by itself while four Conversations are already taking a
place on the server, whatever roadmap or Process they belong to, and starts what
it held back as places come free. The number is a setting, four where nobody has
said otherwise, beside the roadmap's own three — so one roadmap cannot take the
whole server by default. It is there for the machine rather than for the human:
a stage may be a heavy build and a test run, and the hardware is shared by
everything the server runs.

**What the human presses is never held back.** Only what Verkstead starts by
itself is — a stage started by a settle — and a press goes ahead over the limit
and is counted from then on. A place is taken by a Conversation with a session
running or a driver registered, so a stage waiting on an answer and a stage
waiting to join the chain are both holding one, which is the human's choice
rather than a reading of what is running. A Conversation that is Done, Draft or
stopped holds none. What was held back starts when a place comes free, oldest
roadmap first and within a roadmap lowest number first, with nobody pressing
anything.
