# Status button

One place at the top of a Conversation's Timeline pane that draws together
everything about the Conversation's status — the status word and lifecycle
state, the running agent's details, and every control from the actions menu,
Resume included. It replaces the scattered header chrome: the ⋯ menu trigger,
the Done/Closed word, the Blocked/Stopped badge, the Waiting-on-checks label,
the foot-of-pane Resume block and the live-session strip. Alongside it, the
pane head gains the repo as a subtitle beside the branch, and profile settings
gain a picker of known Claude models in place of hand-typed ids.

The shape and every decision here were settled in this Conversation's grilling;
each task file carries the decisions its slice needs.
