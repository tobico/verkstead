# Optional steps and an automatic branch

Three additions settled by grilling. A third Pairing role — Review — chosen on
the setup card with a "No review" row, so the wrap-up's review session runs
under an account of its own or not at all. A "No grilling" row on the Grilling
picker, taking a Brief straight to an inline implementation. And an
automatically chosen branch name: the random name is hidden behind a "Draft"
title, the work's first session renames the branch to something appropriate,
and Verkstead follows the rename instead of repairing it.
