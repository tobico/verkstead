# Design fixes

A sweep of workbench design fixes settled by grilling: the conversation cards
lose their drag handle and their noisy attention treatment, the timeline gains
a sticky strip for the active session and cards that show in both their sticky
spot and their place on the record, notices and badges are restyled, hover
states arrive everywhere, and Closed conversations become archivable — which
first frees the word by renaming answered-Set archiving to "locked". Task list
and roadmap cards gain detail pages, and a mobile navigation bug is fixed.
