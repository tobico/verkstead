# Halt and resume

Interruptions are replaced by something plainer. When driving stops, Verkstead
records a durable **halt** and writes a **Notice** on the Timeline saying what
stopped, why, and the evidence; halts Verkstead decides on fire a web push.
Getting going again is one standing **Resume** in the start-work menu — a
recompute from the lifecycle and the branch that never fails silently and
rebuilds a broken worktree — plus **Stop** and **Force stop** beside Abort in
the conversation menu. The remedies ceremony (the card's three-option sheet,
the open-and-settle state, the interruptions table) is deleted, and stored
Interruption events are migrated into Notices.

Settled during the grilling: restarts auto-resume what circumstance stopped
and skip deliberate halts; a finished manual task leaves the Conversation
stopped; Resume carries no note (steering goes through a manual task); resuming
a Wrapping conversation forgets the fix-attempt counters; human-requested
stops push nothing.
