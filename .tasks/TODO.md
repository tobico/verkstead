# Every stage in flight

The pinned stage card and the roadmap's details pane say **where every stage of
the roadmap is** — *waiting on* a named stage, *waiting for a place*, *in
progress*, *waiting to join*, *halted* or *done* — and each stage in flight
leads to its own Conversation. The card showed *done* and *to do* and centred
its window of five on the first stage not done, which was *where the work has
got to* while a roadmap had one such place; with three stages running it would
show one of them and hide two.

The states come from the server's own reading rather than from the boxes and
rather than from the in-progress annotation: the viewer is told a state and does
not work one out. A roadmap that declares nothing reads as it does today, with
the one stage being worked marked *in progress* — a state it never had before,
there having been no such word to say.
