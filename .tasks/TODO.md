# 05. OpenCode

OpenCode is a backend, and the blocking ask's second holder. A Conversation is
grilled and built under an OpenCode Profile: a blocking `verkstead ask` held
open by opencode's own shell tool, screen-signature idling, the session store
read onto the Timeline, `provider/model` Pairings, and the form offering the
type.

Stages 01 to 04 built the ground and proved it on three backends — the agent
type, the one-home account, the per-type launch line and binary,
screen-signature idle with its byte-quiet long-stop, the Guide tailored per
channel, and a Transcript both named and found. What this stage adds is
OpenCode's own: its XDG-shaped account, its launch line, its idle signature, the
Guide tailored to *it* rather than to its channel, a reader for a session store
that is a SQLite database rather than a file of lines, and the renderer for the
records it keeps.

**Two things this stage stands on that are not in the repository.** There is no
`opencode` on the machine — task 01 puts it there as its first step, which is
the human's answer. A provider account is the second, and the end-to-end proof
needs one.
