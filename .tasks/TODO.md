# Share cleanup

Sharing a Conversation moves out of the status button menu into a Share pane of
its own, opened by an icon button on the timeline pane header; the share viewer
stops being configurable, every link composing through the hosted copy; and an
off-by-default toggle has the wrap-up share the record to the pull requests
when the work settles to Done.

Settled in the grilling: the whole share viewer settings section goes, the
download offer included; the four share rows leave both menus; the pane shows
the viewer link with a copy button, the publish moment and the underlying gist
link; and the auto-share fires only on the wrap-up's own settle to Done, gated
by one recorded fact — a share-to-PR comment was left on this Conversation — so
hand-shared work stays quiet and a failed share gets another go at the next
settle. Failures land as a Notice; success is quiet.
