# Close and the retirements

The control surface reads as the settled model and nothing else. **Abort becomes
Close** everywhere — the same press it always was, renamed to say what it does:
the Worktree deleted, the branch kept, pressable from any state. **Reopen and
Manual Task are retired**, because Steer already does both jobs and does them
better: steering a closed Conversation is the way back in, and a steer into
Implementing with a hand-written instruction drives the pipeline where a Manual
Task left the work standing beside it. **Steer is the one hand on the wheel.**

What is left after that is words. CONTEXT.md, the design document and the
adoption guide still teach the retired vocabulary, and the last task is what
makes the written record and the product say the same thing.
