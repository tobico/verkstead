# The agent's call

The agent moves the work itself. The human ticks, per Conversation, which other
devices of the cluster it may be transferred to — under *May be transferred to*
in the device select's panel while drafting, and in the Transfer dialog after —
the drafting device always permitted. Where that list is not empty, the
session's prompt names those devices with their OS and says when to reach for
`verkstead transfer <device>`, and the Guide carries a section on it. The call
runs the preflight and exits non-zero naming what is missing, refuses a device
off the list, and otherwise records the request and returns; the session is
ended once its turn is over, the Conversation is moved, and the Timeline says
*Transferred to B at the session's request*.

Stage 09 already built the move a human press asks for, and stage 10 the
harness resume that carries the conversation on at the far end — so what this
stage adds is the consent (the ticks), the call, the ending at the turn's end
that a mid-work agent needs, and telling the agent. The demonstration is an
agent on Linux asked to fix a Windows build moving itself to the Windows VM and
carrying on there; in this repository it is proved by the two-server transfer
suite with a stub agent, and the human runs the VM demo once the stage lands.
