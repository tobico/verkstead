# Process on the record

Every Conversation gets a **Process**, and the composer asks for it. A new draft
on the compose page and a saved Draft's composer both draw a **Process** picker
between Repo and the Pairings, offering Develop and nothing else yet, defaulting
to Develop and switchable for as long as the Conversation drafts; it freezes
with the Pairings when the work starts, and from then on the Brief's setup facts
and the details pane say which Process the Conversation is. Every Conversation
from before this reads Develop, except one that adopted a pull request, which
reads Review — read rather than backfilled, so nothing is written into old rows.

Nothing about how Develop runs changes. What this stage delivers is the record,
the wire and the control: the fact of the Conversation that every Process after
it is a variant of, and the one place a later stage adds a row to. All five
variants are here from the start, because the store reads and writes every one
of them and what a later stage brings is a start path, never a variant.
