# The backlog and stage runners across

The last two built-in Activities cross the boundary stage 08 drew: the
**backlog runner**, which works `.tasks/TODO.md` box by box with a `next-task`
session per task and the finish after the last, and the **stage runner**, which
reads a roadmap under `docs/roadmaps/<name>/` and starts every stage that is
ready as a Conversation of its own, up to the limits. With them every Activity
Verkstead runs is a component in the built-in Activities plugin, and stage 11
can write the five Processes as data.

The Activity interface gains what these two need and no other Activity does:
reading the Repo's committed files, placing work by platform, joining a
roadmap's chain, and starting a Conversation, which is a capability declared in
the manifest and shown at install. The stage runner keeps today's shape (Set
1839, Q1): it runs whenever a stage could start, at a settle, a places look, a
press or a home device hearing a report, starts what is ready, and ends. It is
not a long-lived Activity waiting on its stages.
