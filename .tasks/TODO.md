# Collapsing sidebar

The conversations sidebar is a full column at every width that stands it
beside another pane, and on a laptop that column is a quarter of the screen
given to a list the human glances at. Now it collapses. Under 100rem the pane
stands as a 3rem strip — the logo, the new-conversation icon, the first three
characters of each card's title with its mark under them, and a blank foot —
and expands to its full width over the panes beside it under a hover, a
keyboard focus or a tap, with a shadow saying it is over them. From 100rem it
is the full sidebar it is today, at its remembered share, with its divider
draggable again.

The collapsed strip stands at every width under 100rem, phones included, so
the phone's one-pane walk goes with it: the frame fills the window and the
panes scroll inside it everywhere, the second pane joins the strip from 60rem
rather than 80rem, and the timeline's *← Conversations* is gone because the
conversations are always beside it. Width alone decides which the sidebar is;
there is no pin and no manual collapse. Settled in the grilling of 2026-10-19,
Sets 1259, 1262, 1265 and 1270.
