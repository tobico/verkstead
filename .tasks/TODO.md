# 03. Codex

The first backend whole. A Conversation is grilled, built and wrapped under a
Codex Profile: launched from its one home directory, asking by store-and-nudge,
judged idle by the signature it draws at its prompt, its Transcript readable on
the Timeline out of its own rollout log, and a usage-limit stop naming the
account when the phrase is seen. The Profile form offers the `codex` type from
this stage on.

Stages 01 and 02 built the ground — the agent type, the one-home account, the
per-type launch line, screen-signature idle with its byte-quiet long-stop, the
Guide tailored per backend, and store-and-nudge asking. This stage is the first
backend to stand on all of it, and what it adds is Codex's own: its launch
line, its at-the-prompt signature, its rollout log found rather than named, its
renderer, and its usage-limit phrase.

**One thing waits on the human.** There is no logged-in Codex account on the
machine, and tasks 02 and 05 cannot be proven against the real TUI without one
— a ChatGPT subscription or an API key, either of which lands in the home's
`auth.json`. Everything a task can do without one is still its to do; a
criterion that needs the account and cannot get it is recorded as waiting
rather than quietly softened.
