# Steer and stages

Companions can join and open up mid-life, and roadmaps carry them. The Steer
modal gains a companion section on every target work goes on in — Grilling,
Implementing and Wrapping — where a companion is added or a read-only one is
upgraded to read-write. It is sandbox setup for the sessions to come rather
than a property of one state, and it only ever widens the set: nothing is
removed and nothing is downgraded, so what a session was once given is never
taken back mid-Conversation. Adding and upgrading both follow the grill start's
shape — fetch, resolve, cut or detach, bind — including the fetch the steer's
own worktree repair deliberately skips, because a companion joining now is new
work rather than an old checkout being put back.

And a roadmap stage starts with its parent Conversation's companion set already
in place, through the one inheritance funnel that already copies the Pairings.
Read-only companions come across as they are; read-write ones cut a branch of
their own per stage, named after the stage's branch, and stacked on the
predecessor stage's companion branch wherever the stage's own branch stacks.
Without this a roadmap grilled with companions would build without them, stages
having no draft moment of their own.

Demonstrable end to end: a Conversation running with one read-only companion is
steered, gains a second companion read-write and has the first upgraded, and
the session that steer launches can write in both and says so on the Timeline;
and the next stage of a roadmap starts with the whole set checked out on
branches of its own, or starts not at all and says which repository stopped it.
