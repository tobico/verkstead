# One stop

A Conversation is driven or it is stopped, and the workbench says so with one
concept. Halt and Pause merge into a single **Stopped** — one badge, one
Notice, one Resume — and the Hold is retired with nothing in its place. No stop
resumes itself any more: an exhausted usage window writes an ordinary stop
naming the Profile and carrying the reset time as words to show, ends the
session it stopped, and waits for a press like every other.

Demonstrable end to end: exhaust a usage window and press Stop on another
Conversation, and both cards read as the same kind of stopped thing; press Stop
and type into a live Screen, and nothing ends the session or advances the run
under you.
