# File attachments

A Conversation takes files alongside its Brief: dropped onto the composer or
picked through a paperclip beside the presses, drawn as a row of pills under the
text, and copied into a directory of the Conversation's own under the Data
Directory. Every session of the Conversation gets that directory read-only
inside its Sandbox, and its prompt ends with a listing of what is there — so a
screenshot, a spec or a data file the human has to hand reaches the agent
without being pasted into the Brief.

The grilling settled it as a per-Conversation record with an origin, the Brief
being the one origin for now and an Answer the next: a flat directory per
Conversation with same-named files renamed rather than replaced, kept through
Close and Trim and removed only when the Cleanup deletes the Conversation, and
frozen with the Brief. The compose page holds files in the page until the press
and replays them after the Conversation is made. The `CONTEXT.md`
**Attachment** entry is written beside this plan.
