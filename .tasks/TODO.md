# Carrying an inline run and a roadmap's writing on

ADR-0030 made every way a session is cut off a reason to resume that session's
own conversation rather than prime a fresh one from the record — and reads the
occasion in `resume::recompute`, where every way back in passes. Two directions
of an Implementing Conversation are left out of that reading, and a session cut
off part way through either of them still loses everything the agent had in its
head: an **inline** implementation, and a **roadmap**'s own writing.

They were left out for a reason rather than missed. The row the reading writes
is spent by the launch after it, and what each of these two directions answers
— whether the branch is already on a pull request — is asked over the network by
the launch itself and may come out as a wrap-up with no session in it at all. A
row written in the recompute is then a row nothing spends, which the next session
of the work would take up and be told to resume a conversation that had nothing
to do with it. So carrying these on means writing the row where the session is
launched, and deciding what *mid-turn* means for a run with no backlog to read it
off.

Raised by the review of the branch that built ADR-0030, and split out on the
human's answer to it.
