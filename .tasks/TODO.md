# Drafting on a device

A new Conversation is drafted onto whichever device should do the work. The
compose page grows a **device select** left of the Repo select, drawn only where
another device exists, reading the device last picked in this browser and this
device until then. Picking one makes the Repo dropdown list that device's Repos
— Open and Create among its rows — the base picker its branches, the companion
rows its registry and the three pairing pickers its Profiles, all of it through
the Relay. *Start work* then creates the Conversation there and the page lands
on its remote URL, where it reads in the Merged List under that device.

A saved draft's composer carries the same select, and a pick moves the draft:
the Brief, the branch and the attached files replayed onto the other device
against a Repo picked there, and the draft here closed with a Timeline that says
where it went. Demonstrable end to end from A's browser — draft on B, grill on
B, see it in the merged list under B.
