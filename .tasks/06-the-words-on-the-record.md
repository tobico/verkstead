# 06. The words on the record

## What to build

Bring the project's vocabulary and its ADR level with what this stage built.

- `CONTEXT.md`: **Notice** — the *Being revised by ADR-0029* pointer comes off
  and the entry says what is now true: Verkstead writes a Question Set of its
  own as well, where declared work wants a device that is asleep or not yet
  permitted. **Question Set** says a Set may be Verkstead's own, named as its
  asker, settled by a handler of Verkstead's rather than by an agent, and that a
  lock may carry a line. **Permitted Device** says the call on an unticked or sleeping linked device
  is a request. Leave the **Stage** pointer for stage 06.
- ADR-0029 says where its *Verkstead asks with a Question Set of its own* now
  stands, including the decisions this stage's planning took (Set 1547): one
  *Move to* per unticked device; ticked-asleep plus unticked-awake is one Set
  saying both; a device awake but lacking stays a Notice; the wait asks the
  device on a paced clock.
- Anything in `docs/design/verkstead.md` that describes the agent's call as a
  refusal or Notices as Verkstead's only voice.

Use none of the words `CONTEXT.md` lists under *Avoid*.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No *Being revised by ADR-0029* pointer remains on a term this stage made
      true, and the Stage's remains.
- [ ] ADR-0029 records Set 1547's four decisions.
