# 06. The vocabulary this stage adds

## What to build

The glossary catches up with the control this stage built.

**A Steer entry**, written the way its neighbours are — what it is, then the
rules that matter, then an _Avoid_ line. It has to say:

- **What it moves.** Targets are Grilling, Implementing, Wrapping and Done.
  Draft and Closed are not targets, each having its own way in. Sources are any
  state at all.
- **What happens at the click.** The drive stops before the modal opens, a
  running session is seen out unless **Interrupt current task** ends it where it
  stands, and Cancel leaves the Conversation stopped with Resume on offer.
- **What it recreates.** A Worktree from the branch where the directory has
  gone, and the branch itself for a Draft.
- **What it records.** The Steer Event carrying the brief or the instruction,
  beside the machine's own Moved line; the Pairing picked, settled as the
  Conversation's; and the state move itself. Submitting also clears any stop and
  resumes in the same press — except into Done, which is the move alone.

Written against the same states the modal actually offers, and against what the
tasks before it built rather than what this brief imagined.

**And Resume's entry is amended.** It currently sends the reader to Manual Task
for steering the work; that is Steer's job from here. Retiring Manual Task
itself belongs to the stage after this one, so this is the cross-reference alone.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] CONTEXT.md holds a Steer entry naming its targets, its sources, what the
      click does, what it recreates and what it records.
- [ ] Resume's entry sends the reader to Steer rather than to Manual Task for
      steering the work.
- [ ] No entry describes a target the modal does not offer.
