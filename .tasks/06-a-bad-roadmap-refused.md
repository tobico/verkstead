# 06. A bad roadmap, refused at both ends

## What to build

A roadmap whose lines **declare badly** starts nothing, wherever a start is
decided, and says why in the words the judgement already refuses it in — a
declaration on some lines and not others, an `after` naming no stage of the
roadmap, a platform that is not one of the three, or a cycle. Nothing is repaired
and nothing falls back to running in order, which would run a roadmap in a way
nobody wrote down.

Three places, and it is the same sentence in each:

- **A running roadmap**: a settle on it starts nothing and leaves a notice on the
  settling Conversation's Timeline, the way every other *nothing was started* is
  said.
- **The roadmap notice and the compose page** offer nothing off it, the same way
  they offer nothing off a roadmap that has finished — the human cannot press
  something that would be refused.
- **The press** refuses naming the fault, the way it names a branch already
  taken. That is the one path with somebody waiting on an answer, and a roadmap
  written by hand or by the old tools — which is what adoption is for — is the
  likeliest to declare badly, so the fault is named where the press was made
  rather than only on a Timeline nobody has opened.

This is the other half of the refusal stage 01 built for a roadmap's own session
at `verkstead done`; the judgement itself is already written and is not changed
here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A settle on a roadmap that declares badly starts nothing and leaves the
      judgement's own sentence on the Timeline.
- [ ] Nothing is offered off such a roadmap on the roadmap notice or on the
      compose page.
- [ ] The press refuses it naming the same fault, in the same sentence, the way
      it names a branch already taken.
