# 07. The docs

## What to build

The decision is recorded where the ones it amends are. **ADR-0019**, the Code
pane, gains a *Forwards* section: a terminal's ports read on the member while
an attach over the link is held, forwarded by the attaching device on its
loopback at the same number, a taken port skipped and said, the lifetime the
attach's with the watcher's grace, the member connecting only what it reports to
a device attached to that terminal, a printed URL hurrying the read and
forwarding nothing, and no stopping by hand — each with the alternative that was
rejected and why, in the ADR's own voice. **ADR-0020**'s relay section gets a
note that a Forward's connections cross the link as one upgrade each, bridged
by the Relay's own bridge, and that the Forwards reading and Nudge are a
device's own and are not served over the link.

**`CONTEXT.md`** gains a **Forward** entry in the workbench vocabulary: what
one is, who holds it, how it ends, and what it is not — with an avoid-list
keeping *tunnel*, *proxy*, *port forward* as a verb for the Relay, and
*exposing* out of it, and the Relay's own entry pointing at it.

Any comment in the code that still says nothing reads a terminal's processes
beyond the busy check, or that the three attach sockets are the whole of what
the bridge carries, is brought up to date.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] ADR-0019 carries a *Forwards* section recording every decision above with its rejected alternative, and ADR-0020's relay section notes how a Forward crosses the link and what stays on the device.
- [ ] `CONTEXT.md` has a **Forward** entry with its avoid-list, and the Relay entry refers to it.
- [ ] No comment in the server or the viewer still says the busy check is the only process reading or that the bridge carries only the three attach sockets.
