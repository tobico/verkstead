# 02. Edits and removals relayed home

## What to build

Every device's Profiles section lists everyone's, and the form over a **mirror**
saves: the human's choice over editing a Profile only where its account lives.

**A save on B is put to A as an ordinary edit of A's own Profile**, over the
link this device already holds, addressed by the id the mirror records for it
there. What comes back is A's own answer rather than a second opinion composed
here — a name already taken at home is a name already taken, said in the words a
local clash is said in, and the two uniqueness rules stay the home device's to
hold. The mirror redraws off the refreshed row rather than off what was typed.

**A removal is the same hop**, and it takes the Profile off its home device.
Every device's mirror goes on its next refresh and each nulls the pairings that
named it, as a local removal does — so a Profile removed from B leaves a
Conversation on C reading as one nothing has been picked for.

**A home that is not answering refuses the press by name** and writes nothing
here. A mirror edited against a machine that is not there would be a row
disagreeing with the account it stands for, and the relay already refuses a call
it could not make in its own words.

**Nothing is relayed twice.** A device puts the edit to the Profile's home and
nowhere else; a third device learns of it by refreshing its own mirror, which is
the rule every announcement in a cluster is held under.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An edit made on B lands in A's own Profiles, and both devices' rows redraw
      off the saved row.
- [ ] A name that clashes at home comes back as the refusal a local clash gives,
      naming which of the two rules refused it.
- [ ] A removal pressed on B takes the Profile off A and the mirror off every
      device, leaving the Conversations that had picked it with nothing picked.
- [ ] With A not answering, both the save and the removal are refused naming the
      device, and nothing local has moved.
