# 04. Continue a roadmap places its stages

## What to build

The *Continue a roadmap* press starts stages too (`conversations::adopt`: the
pressed draft becomes the lowest ready stage, and each other ready stage starts
alongside it), and every one of them is placed exactly as a settle's start
places it (Set 1549, Q3): somebody is at the workbench, but they pick no device.

Each stage the press starts reads its platform off the same reading the press
already makes, and goes through the same placement as task 02 and 03 — moved
before its planning, asked about with the Set, or planned here with a Notice —
before its first session launches. What the press answers to the button, and the
Notices it writes, say where each one went in the same words a settle's do.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A press on a roadmap whose lowest ready stage is `on windows`, with a
      ticked Windows device awake, moves the pressed Conversation there before
      its planning starts.
- [ ] A stage started alongside the press that is `on windows` is placed the
      same way, and one with no platform starts here as before.
- [ ] Where no Windows device can take it, the press's stages get the same Set
      or Notice a settle's would.
