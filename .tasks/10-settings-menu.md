# 10. Settings behind an ellipsis menu

## What to build

The way into Settings moves from the link at the foot of the conversations
panel into a dropdown menu, opened by an ellipsis button to the right of the
Verkstead wordmark in that panel's header — echoing the ellipsis at the top
of each conversation, and built on the shared menu component from the
previous task. The menu holds just the one Settings entry for now, and the
foot link goes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An ellipsis button sits right of the wordmark and opens a menu whose
      Settings entry navigates to the settings page
- [ ] The "Settings →" link at the panel's foot is gone
- [ ] Works at every pane layout, phone widths included
