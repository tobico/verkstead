# 02. Starting what waited

## What to build

A place coming free starts what task 01 held back, with nobody pressing anything.

Task 01's hold is only half a limit on its own: the places it counts are freed by
a Conversation reaching Done, by a run stopping, by a grilling ending — none of
which is a settle, and the carry-on runs at settles alone. So a stage held for a
place would wait until something else of its own roadmap settled, which may be
nothing at all. What closes the loop is a **look of this server's own**, at the
pace the rest of its sweeps are chosen at: the roadmaps Verkstead is driving are
read again, and what the free places hold is started.

**Nothing about the waiting is stored.** What is ready is read afresh from the
declarations, the record and the boxes at every start, so a look after a restart
finds exactly what the last one did and a restart loses nothing that was waiting.
A second record of *this stage is waiting* could only come to disagree with what
is actually ready.

**A start with no settle behind it needs a predecessor**, the carry-on having
taken its Pairings, its companions, its base and the Timeline it says things on
from the Conversation that settled. It takes them from the **roadmap's chain**
instead: the highest settled stage of it, and the roadmap's own Conversation
where no stage of it has settled yet — which is the foot of the chain either way,
and already what the base is chosen off. The notice that it started goes on that
Conversation's Timeline.

**And one start, however two arrivals overlap.** A settle and a look landing
together must not start the same stage twice; the branch refusal behind every
start is the backstop rather than the plan.

Which one of several goes first is task 03. Here one roadmap, one place, one
stage is the whole of it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With the places full and a stage held, a Conversation reaching Done frees
      one and the held stage starts, with nothing pressed and no stage of its own
      roadmap having settled.
- [ ] A server restarted while a stage was waiting starts it when a place comes
      free, nothing about the waiting having been written down.
- [ ] The started stage gets the Pairings, the companions and the base of the
      highest settled stage of its roadmap's chain — the roadmap's own
      Conversation where none has settled — and that Timeline says it started.
- [ ] A settle and a look arriving together start the stage once.
