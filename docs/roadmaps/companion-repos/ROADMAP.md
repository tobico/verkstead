# Companion repos roadmap

A Conversation can let other registered Repos into its sandbox as **companion
repos**, read-only or read-write — configured beside the branch while the
brief drafts, checked out as Verkstead worktrees of their own, named neutrally
in every session prompt, and, for read-write companions, carried through the
full pipeline: commits on the Timeline, diffs under Question Sets, and a pull
request per touched companion that wrap-up watches to Done. The decisions and
their why are in [the design doc](../../design/verkstead.md) under **Companion
repos**; [CONTEXT.md](../../../CONTEXT.md) gains its terms stage by stage as
each lands.

Each stage is one feature: one branch, one review unit. Task chunkings inside
the briefs are provisional — re-grounded against the codebase when the stage
starts.

Stages 02 and 03 both build on 01 and are reorderable with each other; 04
builds on all three — it steers and inherits what they made.
